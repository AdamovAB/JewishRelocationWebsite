Across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach, families have access to a broad network of synagogues representing Orthodox, Modern Orthodox, Chabad-Lubavitch, Sephardic, Conservative, Reform and other Jewish traditions — alongside Jewish schools, community organizations, kosher restaurants, markets and services.


No state personal income tax. Year-round warm climate. Major international airports with direct flights across North America, Europe and Israel. Florida has adopted legislation specifically addressing antisemitism and incorporating the IHRA working definition.
